The first turn asks about the subject the person brought, with questions that fit it: its situation (what exists today, whether the decision is still open, what it costs to be wrong), the problem and who has it, or what it counts on. Either up to three numbered factual questions, or a single choice with a recommended answer. It asks nothing about who the person is (role, experience, background, link to the market), their preferred format or tooling.
