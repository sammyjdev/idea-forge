# Focus

Read at stage 1. The focus decides where the research and the grill spend their depth.

## Infer it, do not ask it

Read the focus from the first messages: the role the person mentions, their vocabulary, and what they ask for.

| Signal | Focus | Level |
|---|---|---|
| Customers, sales, price, market; no technical words | business | non-technical |
| Users, problem, roadmap, metrics | product | technical words explained |
| Stack, API, schema, latency, versions | technical | technical |
| Screens, journeys, flows, usability | design | non-technical unless they use technical words |
| No signal | product | technical words explained |

State the inferred focus in one line with the first stage 1 question ("I will focus on business; say so if you want another"). Record it in `brief.md` as Focus. It changes whenever the person asks.

## What each focus changes

| Focus | Research questions first | Grill depth | Dossier leads with |
|---|---|---|---|
| business | Market, competitors, pricing, distribution, regulation | Users (who pays), limits (cost), out of scope | Verdict, market evidence, cheaper-than-building options |
| product | Problem, users, substitutes | Users, flow, rules, out of scope | Problem, users, success criteria, candidate features |
| technical | Technology, regulation that shapes data, existing systems | Data, rules, edges, limits | Decisions, door candidates, risks, feasibility findings |
| design | Users, journeys, substitutes | Flow, states in the journey, edge outcomes as screens | Journey, states, open design questions |
| mixed | One question per area | All branches at thorough depth | Verdict, then per-area summary |

Every focus still covers the minimum: the problem sentence, one finding per research question, the verdict. Focus changes depth, never skips the verdict.

## Language

- business and design: product terms, no technical vocabulary unless the person uses it.
- product: product terms, technical words explained in one line.
- technical: technical vocabulary, versions and limits stated exactly.
