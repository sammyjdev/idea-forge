# Writing

Read before writing long prose or any durable file. Applies to every question, summary and file. Profile writing rules are added on top.

## Shape

- First line is the question or the result.
- After an answer, one line on what changed. No recap.
- Numbered lists only for sequences. Five items per list, ranked.
- No preamble, no closer, no "let me know".

## Sentences

- Short sentences, active voice, a named subject.
- One term per concept. Use the `CONTEXT.md` term once it exists.
- Concrete values instead of adjectives: "under 2 s for 95% of requests", not "fast".
- Plain verbs: "is", "has", "uses". Not "serves as", "leverages".
- No hype words: robust, seamless, powerful, crucial, key, enhance, leverage, delve.
- No em-dash or en-dash. Plain hyphen only.
- No bold for emphasis, no emoji.
- Keep a hedge only when the uncertainty is real.

## Files

- Describe what is, not what changed. History goes in commits.
- Sentence-case headings.
- `scripts/validate.ts` flags vague words and dashes in handoff files.
